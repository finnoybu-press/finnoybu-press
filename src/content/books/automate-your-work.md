**Stop doing the busywork. Wire it up once and let AI run it.**

*Automate Your Work with AI* is the practical guide to connecting the tools you already use and handing the repetitive work to automation.

You'll learn to build triggers, multi-step workflows, and AI-powered agents across the three platforms that matter, Zapier, Make, and n8n, with no coding required. The book covers what each platform does best, how to design workflows that actually hold up, where AI agents add real leverage, and the self-hosting and data-control options (especially in n8n) that matter when the work involves company data.

**The 2027 Edition is revised throughout for late 2026:** Zapier's agents moving into the Zap as AI by Zapier, Make's rebuilt AI Agents and the Maia conversational builder, n8n 2.0, connecting your AI assistant to your automations through MCP, a worked cost comparison at current prices, and the EU AI Act's revised timeline.

Written for professionals, operators, and small teams drowning in manual steps who want their tools to talk to each other and their busywork to run itself.
