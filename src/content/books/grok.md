**When the answer has to be current, Grok is built for the moment.**

xAI's flagship assistant draws on real-time knowledge straight from X, so it can speak to what people are saying now, first-hand, not just to what a search engine indexed.

*Grok: The Complete Guide* covers everything Grok does: the Grok 4.x models, DeepSearch for live research, Aurora and Grok Imagine for images and video, voice, the API and its search tools, and the new coding and work agents. It explains The X Subscription that now bundles Grok with X and Cursor, what Grok genuinely excels at, where its limits and biases lie, and how it honestly compares to ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, so you know when Grok is the right call and when it isn't.

**The 2027 Edition is revised throughout for late 2026:** Grok 4.7 and the rest of the 2026 lineup, SpaceX's ownership of xAI, The X Subscription, Grok Build and Grok Bot, the search-tool API, and the image and code-privacy controversies that every user should understand.

Written in plain English for professionals, researchers, and curious users who want the real picture of xAI's assistant, not the marketing version.
