**OpenClaw is the open-source AI assistant that runs on your own computer and works in the chat apps you already use. This book takes you from first install to a deployment you can trust.**

OpenClaw went from a weekend project to one of the most-starred projects on GitHub in a matter of months. It reads your email, manages your calendar, runs commands, browses the web, and answers you in WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, iMessage, and more than twenty other channels. It's model-agnostic, extensible with skills and plugins, and stewarded since July 2026 by the independent OpenClaw Foundation.

That power is also the risk. The *Agents of Chaos* study red-teamed six OpenClaw agents for two weeks and documented eleven ways they went wrong, from obeying strangers to wiping their owner's email setup. This book shows you how to get the benefits without becoming the twelfth case study.

**OpenClaw: Agentic AI for Everyday Work** is the complete guide: how OpenClaw works, how to set it up, how to use it every day, and how to run it safely for yourself, your household, or your team.

## What's inside

**First principles.** What OpenClaw is, how the Gateway, agents, channels, and tools fit together, and why it meets you in messaging apps instead of its own app.

**Setup, end to end.** Choose where to run it, install it, pick a model, connect your first channel, and have your first useful conversation.

**Daily use.** Memory, context, and sessions. Automations that work while you don't. Skills, the Skill Workshop, and ClawHub.

**The builder's branch.** When you need a plugin instead of a skill, the real plugin SDK, bundles from other ecosystems, and publishing.

**Channels and integrations.** Messaging platforms, email and calendar, browser automation, developer tools, and smart home and voice.

**Security that holds.** The difference between guidance in `AGENTS.md` and enforcement in configuration, sandbox backends, exec approvals, pairing and allowlists, and the governance ecosystem.

***Agents of Chaos*, in depth.** The study, its eleven case studies with the controls that address each, a sourced timeline of what changed in OpenClaw during 2026, and a defensive posture for serious use.

**Beyond one user.** OpenClaw's trust model, household, team, and community patterns with real configuration, and operations with OpenTelemetry and Prometheus.

**Reference.** A configuration reference, a plugin SDK quick reference, a cookbook of guidance paired with enforcement, a case-study reference card, a glossary, and verified resources.

Whether you're a curious power user, a developer building on OpenClaw, or the person responsible for running it for others, this book covers the layer you need.

**The 2027 Edition is fully revised and checked against OpenClaw's documentation, release notes, and the published research. It covers OpenClaw 2.0, the OpenClaw Foundation, OpenClaw Enterprise, the ClawHavoc and Claw Chain incidents, and every major release through November 2026.**
