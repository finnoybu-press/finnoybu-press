**More than a translator: a multilingual workplace platform.**

The tool the world trusts for translation has quietly become a complete platform for working across languages. *DeepL: The Complete Guide* shows you everything it can do, and where a human still has to check its work.

This guide covers the full DeepL suite: DeepL Translate, DeepL Write for AI-assisted writing and editing, and real-time Voice for live multilingual conversation. It goes deep on the features that make DeepL a professional tool rather than a quick translator: custom glossaries, tone and style controls, document handling, the API for automation, and the enterprise-grade privacy that lets regulated teams use it with confidence.

**The 2027 Edition is revised throughout for late 2026:** the renamed Individual, Team, and Business plans, voice-to-voice translation with voice preservation and the new Aure voice model, the Developer and Growth API plans with speech-to-text, DeepL Agent, DeepL inside the major AI assistants, and the EU AI Act's revised timeline.

Written in plain English for anyone who works across languages: global teams, localizers, writers, and businesses serving international customers.
