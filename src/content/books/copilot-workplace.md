**Microsoft Copilot is built into the software you already use every day. This is the book that finally explains how to use it.**

Copilot is in Word. It's in Excel. It's in PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, Edge, Windows, and, since 2026, one Copilot app for home and work that you can open right now. Microsoft has shipped one of the most ambitious AI rollouts in tech history, and most of the people who pay for it have no idea what most of it actually does.

This book changes that.

**Copilot: The Workplace AI Guide** is the comprehensive, plain-English guide to Microsoft Copilot, fully revised for 2027: every plan, every app, every feature. Written for real people doing real work, not for IT specialists or AI researchers, it covers what each tool does, when to use it, and how to write prompts that actually produce useful output.

## What's inside

**Get oriented and set up.** What Copilot is (and isn't), how to choose between free Copilot, Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, Premium, and Pro, and the work plans (Copilot Chat, Microsoft 365 Copilot Business, and Microsoft 365 Copilot), and how to set up the merged Copilot app on Windows, Mac, mobile, Edge, and Microsoft 365.

**Master the core skills.** Writing, research with Bing-grounded citations, everyday productivity, and professional use: from drafting emails and proposals to interview prep and trip planning, with concrete walkthroughs.

**Use Copilot inside every M365 app.** Word for documents and proposals. Excel for analysis, formulas, and Python in Excel. PowerPoint for presentations from prompts and source documents. Outlook for email triage, drafting, and calendar help. Teams for meetings, transcription, and channel agents.

**Unlock the rest of the platform.** Cowork for handing off whole jobs. Researcher for cited reports. Pages, Notebooks, and the new Workspaces. Think Deeper for extended reasoning. Image creation. Copilot Vision for screen and camera understanding. Model choice across OpenAI, Anthropic, and Microsoft models, with Auto choosing for you.

**Get better results.** Prompting fundamentals tailored to Copilot's strengths, strategies for long and complex tasks, and output formatting that's ready to paste straight into your work.

**Make it work for your world.** Targeted chapters for students and educators, business teams and admins (including Microsoft 365 Copilot deployment and governance), and developers using GitHub Copilot under its new usage-based billing, plus Copilot Code for building apps by describing them.

**Stay safe and informed.** Honest coverage of what Copilot gets wrong, how Microsoft's commercial data protection actually works, and the ethics of using AI assistance in academic and professional settings.

**75+ ready-to-use prompt templates** organized by category: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, research, and more.

Whether you've been ignoring the Copilot button for a year or you're rolling Copilot out across a team, this is the book that turns "I have a license" into "I actually use it every day."

**Fully revised for 2027: covers the merged Copilot app (Home, Code, and Autopilot), Cowork, Researcher, Office in Copilot, GPT-6.1 and Claude 5.5 models, Microsoft 365 Premium and Pro, Microsoft 365 Copilot Business, Copilot Credits, and GitHub Copilot's AI Credits, through November 2026.**
