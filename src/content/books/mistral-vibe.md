**Europe's open, sovereign answer to the AI giants.**

*Mistral: The Complete Guide* is the plain-English guide to Mistral and to Vibe, the assistant formerly known as Le Chat, now spanning Work, Code, and Chat.

This book covers the full Mistral picture: the Vibe assistant and what it does day to day, the open-weight Mistral model family, and the story that sets Mistral apart, data sovereignty and self-hosting for organizations that can't, or won't, send their data to a US cloud. You'll learn what open weight really means, how to read a model license, when to self-host and when to use the API, and how to deploy with genuine control over your data.

**The 2027 Edition is revised throughout for late 2026:** the trillion-parameter Mistral Large 4 and its new open-weight license, Mistral Small 4, Mistral Studio (formerly La Plateforme), Vibe Code from Slack, the EU AI Act's revised timeline, and the company's record funding round and own data centers.

Written for businesses, developers, and privacy-conscious professionals, especially in Europe and regulated industries, who want a capable AI assistant on their own terms.
