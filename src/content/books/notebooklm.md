**The most powerful research tool Google has ever built, and the one most people still don't know how to use.**

Gemini Notebook (until July 2026, NotebookLM) looks deceptively simple: upload some sources, ask questions, get answers. But under that quiet interface is a tool that can turn a 200-page report into a 15-minute podcast, a stack of research papers into an interactive video, or a year's worth of meeting notes into a searchable, citation-grounded knowledge base.

Most users never get past the first feature.

This book changes that.

**Gemini Notebook: From Sources to Audio Overviews** is the comprehensive guide to everything Google's renamed research tool can do, fully revised for 2027: every Studio output, every source type, every plan. Written for real people doing real research, in plain English, with concrete walkthroughs.

## What's inside

**Get started right.** Choose the right plan (Free, Plus, Pro, or Ultra, with every limit in one table), understand how source-grounded AI differs from ChatGPT, set up your account, and build your first useful notebook.

**Master the sources.** PDFs, Google Docs, websites, YouTube, audio, video, images, your own Google Play ebooks, and a free textbook catalog: every source type, with strategy for which combinations actually produce good answers.

**Unlock the Studio.** Audio Overviews, Explainer, Short, and Cinematic Video Overviews, interactive sites, Mind Maps, Briefing Docs, Study Guides, Flashcards, Quizzes, Infographics, Slide Decks, Data Tables. Every output, explained and demonstrated.

**Go deeper.** Deep Research, the notebook's built-in cloud computer for data analysis, customization, Interactive Audio Overviews, the 1M-token context window, multilingual notebooks, and the workflows that separate casual users from power users.

**Use it for your world.** Targeted chapters for students, researchers, knowledge workers, analysts, and content creators: each with realistic walkthroughs.

**Share and collaborate.** Public sharing, Collections, notebooks inside the Gemini app, and collaborative notebooks for teams, classes, and research groups.

**Stay safe and informed.** Honest coverage of citation drift, the 2026 voice-cloning lawsuit, what Gemini Notebook gets wrong, and the ethics of AI-assisted research.

**60+ ready-to-use prompt templates** organized by category: research, study, briefing, content creation, comparison, and analysis.

Whether you're opening Gemini Notebook for the first time or you've used Audio Overviews and want the rest of the toolkit, this is the only book you need.

**Fully revised for the 2027 Edition: the NotebookLM-to-Gemini Notebook rename, notebooks in the Gemini app, Cinematic and Short Video Overviews, interactive sites, Expert Intelligence, Collections, and the current plans.**
