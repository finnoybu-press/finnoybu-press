**The most important AI skill isn't prompting. It's judgment.**

*AI Literacy for the Workplace* is about the human side of working with AI: knowing what to trust, what to verify, and when not to rely on a model at all.

This book tackles the risks that matter at work (hallucinations, fabricated sources, deepfakes, and automation bias) and builds the verification habits and human-in-the-loop discipline that keep AI useful instead of dangerous. It also covers why this now matters legally: the EU AI Act (Article 4) and NIST guidance have turned AI literacy from a nice-to-have into a workplace requirement, and this guide shows individuals and organizations how to meet that bar.

**The 2027 Edition is revised throughout for late 2026:** the amended Article 4 as adopted in July 2026 and what it does and does not change, a corrected account of how Article 4 is enforced, the EU's new high-risk dates, and the status of NIST's framework revision.

Written in plain English for every professional who uses AI on the job, and for the managers and teams responsible for using it responsibly.
