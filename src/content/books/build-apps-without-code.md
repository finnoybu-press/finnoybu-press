**Describe the app you want. Ship it the same day.**

A new generation of AI app builders turns plain-English descriptions into working software, and *Build Apps Without Code* is the guide to using them well.

This book covers the leading AI builders (v0, Bolt.new, Lovable, and Replit Agent), how each one works, what each does best, and how to take an idea from prompt to deployed app. Just as importantly, it's honest about the limits: where no-code shines, where it strains, and what you really need to know before trusting an AI-built app in production.

**The 2027 Edition is revised throughout for late 2026:** Replit Agent 4 with its design canvas and parallel agents, the rebuilt v0 with branches and pull requests, Lovable Cloud and Bolt Cloud running your back end, every builder's current plans and meters, the lock-in questions bundling raises, and the EU AI Act's revised timeline.

Written for founders, makers, and professionals who want to build real software without a development team, and want a clear-eyed view of what's actually possible.
