**ChatGPT has changed everything, and it keeps changing. This book makes sure you don't miss any of it.**

ChatGPT is the most widely used AI tool in the world, but most people are only using a fraction of what it can do. They type a question, get an answer, and move on. They don't know about ChatGPT Work, Deep Research, Projects, Space, plugins, scheduled tasks, Study Mode, or the dozens of other features that turn ChatGPT from a clever chatbot into a real productivity platform.

This book changes that.

**ChatGPT: The Definitive Guide** is the comprehensive guide to everything ChatGPT can do, fully revised for the 2027 Edition. Written for real people (not developers, not AI researchers), it covers every feature, every plan, and every practical use case in plain English.

## What's inside

**Get started right.** Choose the right plan (Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, or Enterprise), set up your account, understand the GPT-6 model family, and have your first productive conversations.

**Master the core skills.** Writing, research, everyday productivity, and professional use, with concrete walkthroughs you can follow along with.

**Unlock every feature.** Image creation with ChatGPT Images. Documents and code. Pages, slides, and sites in Space. Voice conversations. Data analysis. Web search and Deep Research. ChatGPT Work. Scheduled tasks. Projects. Plugins and connected apps. Memory. Dot, the always-on agent. Every tool, explained and demonstrated.

**Get better results.** Learn the prompting fundamentals that consistently produce great output, strategies for complex multi-step tasks, and how to control formatting for any destination.

**Use it for your world.** Targeted chapters for students and educators, business teams, and creators and freelancers.

**Stay safe and informed.** Honest coverage of what ChatGPT gets wrong, how to protect your privacy, and the ethical questions every user should be thinking about.

**75+ ready-to-use prompt templates** organized by category: writing, research, business, creative, education, productivity, and technical.

**What changed in 2026.** A plain-English account of what was renamed, replaced, or retired, including Agent Mode, Canvas, Sora, Atlas, DALL-E, and custom GPTs, and where to find each capability now.

Whether you're opening ChatGPT for the first time or you've been using it daily and want to finally understand the full toolkit, this is the only book you need.

**Includes coverage of GPT-6, ChatGPT Work, Deep Research, ChatGPT Images, Space, plugins, Dot, Study Mode, and Interactive Learning.**
