**Meet the coding agent that doesn't wait at your desk.**

Powered by OpenAI's GPT-6 models, Codex works wherever you do: in the terminal, inside your editor, in the ChatGPT desktop app, in the cloud, even from your phone. Hand it a task, walk away, and review the finished work from anywhere.

*Codex: The Complete Guide* is the plain-English manual to OpenAI's autonomous coding agent. It takes you from first setup through every surface Codex runs on, then into the workflows that change how software gets built: delegating jobs to the cloud, wiring Codex into GitHub and Slack so it picks up issues and ships pull requests, steering sessions on your own machine from your phone, and reviewing results instead of babysitting a prompt.

You'll learn what Codex does brilliantly, where it still needs a human in the loop, how to set the approvals and sandbox that keep it safe, and exactly where it fits beside Claude Code and Cursor, so you choose the right tool for each job.

**The 2027 Edition is revised throughout for late 2026:** the GPT-6 model lineup, the Codex app's move into the ChatGPT desktop app, the mobile app and Remote, the Chrome extension, auto-review, Codex Security, and the current plans and usage limits.

Written for working developers and technical teams who want leverage, not hype.
