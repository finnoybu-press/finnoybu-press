**Claude is the AI that listens. This book shows you what it can really do.**

People who try Claude for the first time tend to say the same thing: *"Wait, this one actually listens."* It writes with a voice. It reasons through long documents without losing the thread. It pushes back when something is off. And now it does whole jobs: it researches, builds the deck, drafts the document, and remembers how you like things done.

But most users are still treating it like a fancier search box.

**Claude: The Definitive Guide** is the comprehensive, up-to-date guide to everything Claude can do in 2027, written for real people, not developers, not AI researchers. Every feature, every plan, every practical use case, in plain English.

## What's inside

**Get started right.** Pick the right plan (Free, Pro, Max, Ultra, Team, or Enterprise), set up your account across web, phone, and desktop, understand the models (Fable, Opus, Sonnet, Haiku) and the effort setting, and have your first real conversations.

**Master the core skills.** Writing, research and learning, everyday productivity, and professional work, with concrete walkthroughs from professional bios to two-week trip plans to job-interview prep.

**Unlock every feature.** Vision (photos, PDFs, screenshots, handwriting). Files, connected apps, skills, and plugins. Claude Docs, Slides, Design, Sheets, Whiteboards, and Data. Projects and memory. Research. Handing Claude whole tasks, scheduling them, and letting it work in your browser.

**Get better results.** Prompting fundamentals, long and complex tasks, output formatting and control with XML tags: strategies tuned to the way Claude actually thinks.

**Use it for your world.** Targeted chapters for students and educators, business teams, developers, and creators and freelancers, including when to use Claude vs. ChatGPT vs. Gemini.

**Stay safe and informed.** Honest coverage of hallucinations, knowledge cutoffs, privacy and data use, academic integrity, and the ethics of AI assistance.

**75+ ready-to-use prompt templates** organized by category: writing, research, business, creative, education, productivity, and technical.

Whether you're opening Claude for the first time or you've been using it daily and want to finally understand the full toolkit, this is the only book you need.

**Includes coverage of Claude Fable 5.2, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Haiku 5.5, memory, Research, Claude Docs and Slides, connectors and skills, Claude Code, the mobile and desktop apps, and every major 2026 feature.**
