**The board is asking. The auditors are coming. Your insurance carrier just added an AI exclusion. And the deadlines you planned around just moved. You need a governance program that survives all of it.**

Most organizations now use AI, and nearly all say they have AI governance policies. Far fewer say those policies work: in one 2026 survey of senior leaders, 87% reported a formal framework and only 22% said it worked in practice. The Air Canada chatbot ruling, the Samsung code leak, the Replit production-database wipe, the Arup $25 million deepfake fraud: the incident library is no longer hypothetical.

**AI Governance for Practitioners** is the actionable playbook for the people who actually have to write, defend, and operate AI policy. It is not theory. It is not a survey of every framework ever written. It is a concrete program design that a CISO, compliance officer, risk manager, CPO, or SMB executive can start implementing on Monday morning.

## What's inside

**The governance imperative.** Why AI governance matters now, with sourced data on the governance gap and a clear account of what changed in 2026.

**Frameworks and regulations.** The EU AI Act as amended by the 2026 Digital Omnibus (new high-risk dates of December 2027 and August 2028, new prohibitions, revised AI literacy, small mid-cap relief), the NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, the Council of Europe Convention, Singapore's agentic AI framework, Korea's AI Basic Act, and the contested US landscape, including Colorado's replacement law and the federal push against state AI laws.

**Risk and program design.** Risk classification that stays current when rules change, a 90-day minimum viable governance program, agent inventories, and human oversight that people do not route around.

**Policy, vendors, and compliance.** An acceptable use policy with clauses for agents and delegated authority, vendor assessments that cover training defaults and agent metering, and audit readiness on the amended EU timeline.

**Sector focus and the future.** Financial services, healthcare, legal, and government, plus agentic AI governance, the standards acceleration, the insurance reckoning, and designing for regulatory volatility.

**Templates that work.** A complete AI Acceptable Use Policy, risk assessment, vendor scorecard, incident response plan, compliance checklists on the amended dates, decision trees, and a resource directory.

**11 chapters. Appendices A through G. SMB Spotlights and Action Items throughout. Designed so an executive can read it and start standing up a program the next week.**

**The 2027 Edition is revised throughout for late 2026: the EU AI Act as amended, Colorado's new law, US federal preemption efforts, new Asia-Pacific laws, agent governance, and sourced survey data.** Cross-references to *Securing Agentic AI (2027 Edition)* for the technical depth behind the policies.
