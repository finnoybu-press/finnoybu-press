**The model that broke the cost curve. The only book that explains it end-to-end.**

In December 2024, a small Chinese AI lab released DeepSeek-V3: a frontier-class language model trained for under $6 million when its Western competitors spent $50 million or more. A month later they released R1, a reasoning model that rivaled OpenAI's o1 at a fraction of the cost. In 2026, DeepSeek-V4 and then V4.1 Flash pushed the cost curve lower still, with open weights, a 1-million-token context window, and quality close to the best Western models on most real workloads, while the price list itself learned to change with the time of day.

DeepSeek didn't just release a model. It changed what AI is supposed to cost.

**DeepSeek: Code, Math, and Reasoning** is the comprehensive guide to everything DeepSeek, fully revised for late 2026: the chat app, the API, the models, the deployment options, and the strategic implications for teams already paying OpenAI or Anthropic.

## What's inside

**Understand the family.** V4.1 Flash (the new default), V4 Pro, the retired names, the R1 line, the distilled models (1.5B to 70B), Janus, Prover-V2, Math-V2, and OCR: every model, what makes each distinct, and which one to use when.

**Use the web app.** chat.deepseek.com end-to-end: Instant and Expert modes, file and image uploads, web search, and how to work with code in an app that doesn't run it. Plus the mobile apps.

**Build with the API.** Authentication, peak and off-peak pricing, model selection with rolling model names, prompt caching (the 80% cost saver), tool calling, built-in web search, JSON mode, FIM completion, and the OpenAI, Responses, and Anthropic formats. Every feature with working code.

**Master reasoning.** Thinking on by default, the effort dial, agent workflows, DeepSeek Harness, and the patterns that make reasoning cost-effective.

**Self-host if you need to.** Ollama for laptops, vLLM, SGLang, and BentoML for production. The hardware reality for V4, V4.1, and the distilled models, and the deployment paths that work.

**Use it for your world.** Targeted chapters for developers, researchers, and cost-conscious teams: including DeepSeek-as-a-cost-layer strategies for orgs already on OpenAI or Claude.

**Stay informed.** Honest coverage of the China question, what DeepSeek censors, the US government restrictions through October 2026, and how to evaluate whether DeepSeek fits your team's risk profile.

**60+ ready-to-use prompt templates** organized by category: coding, reasoning, agents, structured output, and cost-optimized cache-friendly patterns.

Whether you're a developer evaluating DeepSeek for the first time, a tech lead building a multi-model strategy, or a researcher running large-scale prompt experiments, this is the only book you need.

**The 2027 Edition covers V4.1 Flash and V4-Pro-0813, peak and off-peak pricing, native vision, the Search, Fetch, and OCR APIs, DeepSeek Harness, and every DeepSeek release through November 2026.**
